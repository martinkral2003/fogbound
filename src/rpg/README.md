# FOGBOUND: HERO — contract for the RPG mode

A second game mode that reuses the island (world.js), the models/fog/lighting, and ideas from the warlord mode, but plays like **Gothic / Skyrim / Fallout meets Hunger Games**:
you are ONE hero (third person over the shoulder, first-person toggle on V) on the dark, foggy island. You scavenge gear, level skills and fight monsters, quest-givers and ~9 rival heroes while the **Dread Fog closes in** until one hero remains. A neutral **settlement (Harrowmere)** is the hub: merchants, a smith, an alchemist, a skill trainer, quests.
The old warlord/RTS mode stays untouched (src/sim.js, ai.js, render.js, ui.js, controls.js, main.js, data.js are the WARLORD mode; do not break them).

## Match flow (owned by flow.js)
1. **Landing (0:00-0:45)**: everyone (you + rivals) starts on a ring of spawn points around the coast with a wanderer kit (rusty weapon, rags, 2 potions). No PvP damage between heroes during the first 45 s ("the calm").
2. **Scavenge**: chests (tiers 1-3, some locked: lockpick/key), camps with guarded loot, hill-fort camps, stronghold ruins (the 4 flat stronghold plateaus at world.hearts: NW one is the settlement site, the other three are loot-rich ruins), shrines, ground pickups, corpses. Monsters (reuse MONSTERS: skeleton, skelArcher, ghoul, wolf, orcRaider, bogBeast, wraith, troll, crypt knight, boss Drowned King at the temple) guard the good stuff.
3. **Dread Fog (the zone)**: first shrink starts at 4:00. 5 stages, circle centre = a random point within 60 m of the temple (0,0) fixed at match start (shown on the map), radii 230 -> 170 -> 115 -> 65 -> 25, shrink durations 60-90 s with holds of 60 s in between; damage outside per second (% max HP) 2, 4, 8, 15, 25. Outside the circle the fog is thick and red-tinted (renderer reads st.zone).
4. **Supply offerings**: at 6:00, 10:00, 14:00 a beam marks a crate with epic/legendary loot somewhere inside the next circle (announce 'Offering of the Fog').
5. **Win**: last hero alive wins; the human gets a placement (#1 = winner) and stats (kills, level, time). Dead humans can spectate. Rivals have permadeath. The match also has quests (settlement) for early power, not required.
6. **Truce at the settlement**: attacking or casting hostile spells inside the settlement radius is disabled (combat.js checks `st.settlement.safe` and distance) for heroes; monsters do not enter. This keeps the hub a safe market until the fog reaches it.
