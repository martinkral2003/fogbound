# CLAUDE.md — FOGBOUND (working title): dark-fantasy warlord island RTS/RPG

Source of truth for game decisions. The previous "TRIBE" stone-age sandbox was scrapped and wiped on 2026-10-08.

## Game decisions (do not re-litigate)
- **Genre:** dark-fantasy (Gothic / Risen mood) **hero-RPG + fortress/RTS hybrid** on **one big foggy island**. Four evil lords fight for domination.
- **Platform:** **desktop browser, 3D (Three.js r170, vendored in `lib/`)**, plain ES modules, **no build step**, static server (`node tools/serve.js`). Mouse + keyboard.
- **Players:** **1–4**. Each human is one lord; empty slots are **AI lords**. Competitive free-for-all (2v2 teams later). Demo = 1 human + 3 AI.
- **Lords (team id = faction id = start corner):** 0 Necromancer (NW, moor), 1 Orc King (NE, highlands), 2 Bandit Leader (SE, deadwood), 3 Cult of Fanatics (SW, ruined abbey).
- **Hero** (direct control, ARPG combat): basic attack, **2 skills**, a **charged spell** (hold, release at cursor; big AoE, huge vs buildings = how defenses fall), and **teleport home** (channelled, interrupted by damage/move, cooldown). Revive on a **timer; gold speeds it up**. No lieutenants, no corruption pacts.
- **Servants:** each lord may hire up to **10** (bandits/skeletons/orcs/zealots). They level up (max 6) and pick up/equip gear. No formations, no counter system. Stances: follow / hold / attack-move.
- **Economy:** **gold only.** Sources: heart trickle, captured gold mines, obelisks, camp/kill bounties, chests. Sinks: servants, buildings, revive speed-up.
- **Building:** free-form placement (rotation + soft snapping), **defense-focused**: wall, gate (own team passes), tower, ballista, watchtower, banner. **Territory influence** gates building and mine income. Buildings stamp a coarse 2 m nav grid; enemy buildings are walkable-at-cost in A* so units path "through" and attack them.
- **Map:** one island, 400×400 m, 4-fold rotational symmetry for fairness. Lord strongholds on the diagonals, **Sunken Temple** boss + ritual in the centre (entrances on the diagonals), mires N/S, ridges E/W. Features: gold mines, obelisks (capture points), monster camps (respawn), chests, shrines, loose gear. **Real fog** (exp fog + drifting mist; fog of war hides enemies).
- **Win:** destroy all rival hearts, or hold the Sunken Temple for 240 s (ritual). Match ≈ 30–45 min.
- **Netcode (later):** host-authoritative `state + step(dt, inputs)` (already how `src/sim.js` is written), MQTT rooms like `../gamee`. Sim is deterministic-ish and has no DOM/Three imports (runs in Node).
- **Audio:** synthesized Web Audio, not started.

## Layout
- `src/util.js`, `data.js` (all tuning/content), `world.js` (terrain + features), `nav.js` (grid + A*), `sim.js` (state+step), `ai.js` (AI lords, produces the same inputs as a human), `models.js` (procedural meshes), `render.js` (Three scene, fog, fx), `ui.js` (DOM HUD, minimap, menus), `input.js`, `main.js`.

## Design v0.3: Warlord rework (agreed 2026-10-10; PARTLY SUPERSEDED by v0.4 below: no bases, Dark Tower, research, region ownership or lord factions; map, perks, aimed skills, dungeons, POIs, waystones, raids-as-attacks, plates are kept). Contract: `src/V3.md`
Why: playtest said the game is too easy and shallow (grab a chest, rush the nearest heart; gold, building, skills and the big area barely matter; skills are click-click). Decisions:
- **Dark Tower** replaces the Heart (internal building id stays `heart`): a strong defender, upgraded by research. It cannot be damaged until you capture its 3 home districts (zone-by-zone conquest). Win = destroy all rival Dark Towers. **No Sunken Temple / ritual.**
- **Research** (gold + time at the Tower, plus points from captured libraries/ruins) unlocks siege servants (rams, catapults), tower upgrades, servant tiers. No siege spell: the charged spell R is strong vs units, weak vs buildings. Defence building stays important and effective; maps give natural chokepoints so castles need little wall building.
- **Regions**: capture zones/mines in the standard way. Owned regions develop automatically through 4 stages (dark houses, roads appear), spawn patrolling guards, pay income; lost regions burn. Roads connect regions to the tower.

## Design v0.7 (agreed 2026-11-01, Q&A over 4 rounds): two-faction war + MQTT co-op/competitive. Builds on v0.5/v0.6/v0.6b (same top-level src/ code, NOT src/rpg). Goal: play this with a friend over MQTT, co-op vs demons OR competitive with heroes split across two human factions.
Pitch: you (+ an allied human over MQTT, + AI filling empty slots) explore a ~300 m island with tombs/dungeons, kill monsters, level up, learn skills, recruit allies. A war against demons is always running in the background; a second human faction can optionally be an enemy instead of an ally, fighting both you and the demons for the same cities. Clearing a city of its defenders hands it to your faction and it starts producing soldiers that march on the enemy; how well you personally fight (camps cleared, towns saved, elites/bosses killed) is what makes that army bigger. Decisions below are FROZEN like other Design sections; build the plan at the bottom instead of re-asking these.
- **Modes:** co-op (today's v0.5/v0.6 game, unchanged: all heroes allied, team.faction all 0, demons are the only enemy) and **competitive** (`st.war = true`): heroes split into faction 0 / faction 1, 2 heroes per faction (today's 4 hero teams, just grouped), demons are a true third side hostile to both. Humans pick a faction in the lobby; AI fills whatever hero slots are left on either side. Same-faction humans = co-op-vs-both-threats; opposite-faction humans = 1v1 war with an AI teammate each.
- **Hostility (competitive only):** heroes of different factions become hostile to each other (same combat/damage/skill system as vs demons, no separate PvP tuning for now — retune from playtest feel, not in advance). Demons stay hostile to all living teams. Same-faction heroes/servants never fight each other, same as today.
- **Map:** triangular layout on the existing ~300 m generator: faction 0 capital, faction 1 capital, and the demon fortress/lands each anchor one corner/third of the island (not the old fixed SW-capital/NE-fortress diagonal). Between the three corners: several ordinary flippable towns (the economy/territory prize) PLUS the old "middle of the map" content scaled up — multiple tombs/dungeons, a few bosses, and contested side-events — rather than just one race-boss. In co-op mode (no second faction) the layout degrades gracefully to one capital + the demon corner, i.e. today's map.
- **City capture:** a town is "owned" by whichever faction's people currently hold it (`t.owner`, new field; demons use the existing safe/besieged/fallen machinery unchanged). A rival faction takes it by clearing out its defenders/garrison (heroes + that faction's levy squad), same moment-of-clear trigger as today's camp-clear logic — no capture timer, no separate HP pool. A city falls to DEMONS exactly as today (burns, loses services) regardless of who owned it; it is only ever CAPTURED by a living rival faction's presence.
- **City defense investment:** at an owned city, heroes can spend gold to upgrade its walls/tower HP and tower damage (tiers, like the old Forge), and/or leave servants/followers garrisoned there — both raise that city's defense rating against demon raids and rival sieges. This is the lever CLAUDE.md v0.6b's "guard up" idea above was missing: investment is a real choice, not automatic.
- **Per-faction levy armies ("soldiers march to the enemy"):** src/levy.js's reserve/squad system (already built, currently single-faction, defensive-only: relief + patrol) gets two additions, in order: (1) **offensive musters** — once the reserve is deep and no own town is besieged, send a bigger squad at the nearest open demon gate / fortress heart instead of only patrolling (this works today even in plain co-op, no faction split needed — build this FIRST, it's the most direct, lowest-risk piece of the brief). (2) once competitive mode exists, **duplicate the levy per faction** (two independent reserves/squad lists keyed by faction id, militia teams split from today's single team 4) so each faction's own hero deeds grow its own army, and that army marches on whichever enemy (demons or the rival faction's nearest city) is the current objective.
- **Demons as the third side:** opportunistic raiders — mostly held near their own corner/fortress, launching the existing assault-wave logic (horde.js) at whichever human-owned city is currently weakest-defended/nearest, instead of a single fixed capital. A city's defense investment (above) is the direct counter.
- **Death/respawn (competitive):** respawn only at a waystone your own faction currently owns (same timer as today); killing an enemy hero feeds the killer's faction levy a small amount, mirroring today's `LEVY.heroDeath` drain, so hero deaths matter to the war, not just to the dying player.
- **Win/lose (competitive):** destroy the enemy faction's capital (take their other cities first, then break the capital's walls/heart) to win. Demons remain a real threat throughout — the existing capital-falls doom clock still applies independently per faction, so a faction can lose to demons even while "winning" the human war.
- **Multiplayer (MQTT):** NOT host-broadcast after all (superseded below, 2026-11-01) - `st` is too big to snapshot every tick, so it's deterministic LOCKSTEP instead: both peers run the full sim locally and only exchange inputs. First pass scope: **you always host**, your friend types in a 4-letter room code to join, AI fills the other two hero slots. Reuses `st.humans`/`opts.humans` which ALREADY supports more than one human-controlled team.

### MQTT multiplayer: actually built (2026-11-01, user asked directly for this + GitHub Pages deploy)
Rewrote `src/net.js` from the row-8 stub above into **deterministic lockstep**, not host-broadcast: both peers run the full `createGame()`/`step()` simulation locally and exchange ONLY player inputs (never game state), because `st` (nav grid, every building/unit) is too big to snapshot every tick. This works because the sim never calls raw `Math.random()` outside render-only code (grepped). Protocol: topics `fogbound-<room>/{lobby,join,leave,start,cmd,sync}` on the public broker `wss://broker.emqx.io:8084/mqtt` (mqtt.js loaded via CDN `<script>` in index.html, pinned `mqtt@5.16.1`); host gets a room code, publishes `start` once a friend joins, both then `createGame()` with those exact options. Each peer publishes its own hero's input a few ticks ahead (`DELAY=9`, batched 3-at-a-time with a 6-tick overlap window so one lost/late MQTT message doesn't stall the match - the public broker measurably drops/throttles a naive one-message-per-tick-per-peer stream, see below) and only steps the sim once every human team's input for that tick has arrived.
- **Wired in:** `index.html` (`#mpRow` Host/Join/room-code UI inside the existing menu, `#mpStatus` line, the mqtt.js script tag), `main.js` (`hostOnline`/`joinOnline`/`roomCode`/`mpStatus`/`applySync`, `start()` now takes an optional `online` param and both `createGame()` + the `loop()` stepping branch on `game.online`).
- **Found and documented, NOT fixed - the sim is not actually bit-exact deterministic:** built `tools/v7-netplay-test.mjs` (two real MQTT peers, in-process, against the live public broker) to verify the protocol; it reaches the end with 0 stalls (every input delivered and applied at the right tick - **the networking protocol itself works**) but the two peers' final game states differ by about a meter of hero position after 8 seconds. Isolated with `tools/v7-determinism-test.mjs` (zero networking at all: just two local `createGame(same seed)` + identical scripted input runs in one process) that this is a **pre-existing bug in sim.js/ai.js**, not something this session's networking work caused: one AI-controlled team takes a measurably different path between two otherwise-identical runs. Ruled out: `Math.random()` (none outside render-only files), `Date.now()`/`performance.now()` (none in sim/ai files), the one `.sort()` in `ai_combat.js` (target-by-distance - it's a stable sort). Root cause still open.
- **Mitigation shipped, not a fix:** `net.js` exports `publishSync()` - the host broadcasts the 4 heroes' `{x,z,hp}` every 3 sim-seconds (tiny payload), and `main.js`'s `applySync()` hard-snaps the client's local copies to match. This bounds visible hero drift to a few seconds' worth instead of letting it compound for a whole match, but does NOT correct monster/servant/building positions - full-world drift is still possible over a long match.
- **Verified:** `node --check` clean on every touched file; `node tools/v7-netplay-test.mjs` - real two-peer run over the live public broker, 0 stalls, protocol delivery confirmed, fingerprint mismatch is the known bug above (test explains this in its own failure output rather than looking like a mystery regression); `node tools/v7-determinism-test.mjs` - isolates the same bug with zero networking.
- **NOT verified:** two actual browser tabs. Everything above was tested with real MQTT traffic but synthetic scripted "players" in Node - the browser-side UI wiring (`hostOnline`/`joinOnline`, the room-code input, `applySync` reading `game.st`) has only been read over, not click-tested. Do this together: open the page in two tabs (or two devices), Host in one, Join with the code in the other.
- **Next, if picked up again:** (a) root-cause the sim/ai.js nondeterminism properly (start from `tools/v7-determinism-test.mjs`'s repro - try disabling AI teams one at a time, or binary-search which of ai_combat.js/ai_plan.js/ai_kit.js/perks.js/servants.js is involved) so lockstep can be trusted bit-exact instead of leaning on `publishSync`'s band-aid; (b) a real lobby (host-or-join symmetry, >2 humans, reconnect handling) instead of today's you-always-host 2-player pass; (c) click-test the actual UI in two tabs.
